This is a webpage about fruit. Listing their color and their peak growing season within the U.S.

Type a slash with "fruits" in the html bar above to get json data. Type a slash after "fruits" with a fruit name to get info about a specific fruit (i.e. "/fruits/banana"). Info is also listed below.

  • Apple
    • Color: varies from green, yellow, to red
    • Season: late summer to late October
  • Orange
    • Color: orange
    • Season: varies in time but typically from December to March
  • Grapes
    • Color: purple
    • Season: August through October
  • Cherry
    • Color: red or yellow
    • Season: May through July
  • Watermelon
    • Color: pink and green
    • Season: May through September
  • Blackberry
    • Color: black
    • Season: Late spring through the end of summer
  • Raspberry
    • Color: red
    • Season: June through September
  • Peach
    • Color: peach
    • Season: May through early September
  • Cantaloupe
    • Color: orange
    • Season: June through August
  • Banana
    • Color: yellow
    • Season: year round
  • Strawberry
    • Color: red
    • Season: February through July
  • Blueberry
    • Color: blue
    • Season: varies but typically from April to September
